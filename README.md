# LADE LUXURY TRENDZ

> Premium Nigerian Fashion & Lifestyle Boutique Website

A complete, modern luxury digital fashion experience designed for **Lade Luxury Trendz**, translating the atmosphere of high-end boutique retail (deep espresso woodwork, polished marble reflections, warm linear and chandelier lighting, and champagne/gold accents) into an editorial digital fashion house.

---

## 🏛️ Website Architecture

The project consists of four main pages:
1. **HOME (`index.html`)**:
   - Cinematic Hero Slider (Slider Revolution-style with Ken Burns slow zoom, layered text animations, and progress bar)
   - Brand Story Introduction (*The Art of Elevated Style*)
   - 6 Featured Collections (Women's, Men's, Shoes, Handbags, Jewelry & Accessories, Ready-To-Wear)
   - Signature Selection with interactive category filters, Quick View modal, and dynamic currency switcher (₦ NGN / $ USD)
   - The 4 Pillars of Lade (*Why Lade*)
   - Services Preview (*More Than Fashion*)
   - Cinematic Parallax Statement
   - Store Experience Showcase featuring the physical flagship boutique
   - Customer Testimonials Carousel
   - Editorial Instagram / Social Gallery
   - Final Dramatic CTA & Luxury Footer
2. **ABOUT US (`about.html`)**:
   - The Story Behind Lade Luxury Trendz
   - Our Story, Vision & Mission
   - The Four Core Values: Quality, Elegance, Individuality, Exceptional Service
   - What Makes Us Different
   - The Lade Experience
3. **OUR SERVICES (`services.html`)**:
   - 8 Dedicated Luxury Services (Personal Styling, Custom Outfits, Wardrobe Curation, Fashion Consultation, Premium Shopping Experience, Gift Selection, Accessories & Complete Looks, Special Occasion Styling)
   - The 4-Step Atelier Protocol (Tell Us Your Style &rarr; Receive Recommendations &rarr; Select Your Pieces &rarr; Complete Your Look)
   - Direct Styling Concierge CTAs
4. **CONTACT US (`contact.html`)**:
   - Flagship Contact Details (Address, Phone, WhatsApp, Email, Opening Hours)
   - Interactive Consultation Inquiry Form with luxury focus states
   - Instant WhatsApp Styling Advice Card
   - Interactive Styled Dark Luxury Map UI

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser or run a local static server:

```bash
# Using Python
python -m http.server 8080

# Or open index.html directly
start index.html
```
